# 14 · Class library

Every class and every skill in the game. The rules these rows follow are in `13-units-classes-power.md`: the slots and budgets (§11), the conditions (§6), the proficiencies (§8) and the content rules (§19). Every table here whose first column is **ID** is data, checked by `node docs/tools/content-check.mjs`.

---

## 1. How to read it

**Class tables**

| Column | Holds |
|---|---|
| ID | `class.<name>` |
| Class | the class's name on screen |
| Family | one of the 11 civilian families, or Military |
| Proficiency | the trade that opens its skills (13 §8) |
| Attributes | its two key attributes, the first one first (13 §4.1) |
| Health | Health per level (13 §4.2) |
| Gear | what it works and fights with, and the heaviest armour it wears |
| Works at | where its class work happens |
| Resolve | how it earns Resolve: Work, Fight or Aid (13 §11.3) |
| Deed | its deed (13 §10.4) |
| Also trains | other proficiencies its work trains, at a quarter of the rate (13 §8) |
| Since | the milestone that ships it |

Advanced classes have a shorter table: their base class, their gear, and when they ship. They inherit everything else from the base.

**Skill tables**

| Column | Holds |
|---|---|
| ID | `skill.<class>.<slot>` |
| Slot | Knack, Active, Ultimate, Art, Mastery, Cataclysm or Office |
| Name | the skill's name on screen |
| Cost | what it spends: `15 stamina`, `20 mana`, `100 Resolve`, an item; nothing for a passive |
| Ready | `Passive`; a cooldown such as `30 s`; `Resolve` for an Ultimate |
| Reach | Self, Target, Point, Direction, Aura or Blocks, with its distances (13 §11.4) |
| Tooltip | the line the player reads (13 §19) |
| Rules | exactly what it does. Numbers are for a Common user at the proficiency that opens it; ◆ scales with Power (13 §11.7); "of a hit" follows 13 §5.1; conditions are in **bold** |
| Since | the milestone that ships it |

Every military class wears its armour and carries its weapons from the kingdom's stores (the best tier on hand). Its class work is drill at a training yard (13 §7.3), patrols and fighting.

### 3.5 Cataclysms

A Calamity's Cataclysm is the row whose **For** names its class; failing that, its base class; failing that, its family. Cataclysms follow the budget in 13 §11.10: each wind-up (up to 3 s) can't be interrupted; "every creature" means friend and foe, "foe" means foes only, terrain falls on whoever is under it, and the Calamity is never hurt by its own (13 §11.9). Every damaging Cataclysm also deals 200,000 siege damage once to each Warden in its area, doubled by Sunder (13 §15.1).
