# HELL-1 hook framing

One fixed yaw adjustment is selected for the HELL-1 postcard after a complete image comparison. It makes an existing hooked formation readable without changing terrain. The catalogue change is under repository verification and is not yet live.

## Why this view

A bounded anatomy audit found active production paths for every required thorn mechanism. A follow-up examined only the 28 already-recorded parents in the old HELL-1 view. Its sole intact hooked main, owner 10263893, had a projected return at the left image edge; the complete main allowance did not fit. That probe stopped as limited absence with no extra rays or owner search. It did not establish a missing shape implementation.

The new direction comes from the 48 saved above-base contour stations. A single analytic solution brings their conservative allowance inside a 12-pixel margin. The minimum yaw adjustment is 9.474928777823404 degrees, rounded upward once to 9.48 degrees. No alternative location, pitch or yaw sweep is tried.

- Eye remains `(5669.5, 30.62, 5417.5)`.
- Target changes from `(5705.6371182972825, 36.21927446565914, 5470.321479354219)` to `(5713.843472103851, 36.21927446565914, 5463.648201284286)`.
- Pitch 5 degrees, FOV 70, time 17.25, radius 512, Normal view, fog and shadows are unchanged.
- The complete target-feature set and named fissure 1088348037 remain unchanged.

The derived contour is a framing aid, not a continuous-mesh certificate or a visibility claim. Actual source validation and rendered pixels decide the next steps.

## Opened-image decision

Root opens both originals. The new frame retains the left hooked tip and separates its upright shaft from the shorter curling formation beside it. The very large central blade and distant fork remain visible. The old view gives the right-side lava fissure more emphasis and shows another inward-leaning blade on the right; that is a real tradeoff.

A fresh image-only Astra reviewer sees anonymous copies, prefers A, and identifies the complete left hook and clearer rooted-form separation as the stronger overall composition. A maps to the candidate. The reviewer also records B's stronger lava-depth cue and additional right-hand blade. No second reviewer is used to change that judgment.

This is a visual-preference decision, **not a formal rubric score or a phase pass**. HELL-1 remains marked ungraded. Seeds 2/3 and the current calibration requirement remain open; no source generation, shader, save identity, camera lens, phase threshold or distant-LOD policy changes.
