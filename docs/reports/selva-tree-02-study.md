# Selva tree 02 · isolated asset study

The second giant-tree form passes its bounded prototype review. It remains outside the live world: forest placement, distance rendering, wind, harvesting and underground roots are future work.

Seven focused tests pass. The fresh one-metre voxel bake records 2,677 wood voxels, 16,938 leaf voxels, 10,188 quads and zero detached voxels. The tree reaches roughly seventy metres above ground. The original tree remains the default; its preserved parameter arrays and source projection are separate evidence from this new bake.

Root and a fresh Astra reviewer opened all six original 1280 × 960 images: `whole-engine.png`, `whole-clay.png`, `crown-engine.png`, `close-engine.png`, `close-clay.png` and `overhead-engine.png`. The unequal crown masses, supporting fork and broad roots establish a distinct giant form. The overhead view keeps the avatar legible at the root base.

Acceptance is limited to the asset's form. Wide views are strongly washed out by haze; the root steps look regular, and the clay views show triangular shading. Final materials and overall appearance need further work. No special light or exposure correction was introduced for this asset. The older tree-01 pictures are historical references, not a matched comparison under the current renderer.

Eight source bindings and all six image hashes match the captured receipt. The render command exits zero after awaiting browser and server closure. There are no recorded application errors. This does not establish performance in a forest or complete regional acceptance.
